## Deferred recommendations (prose only)

- c03: the edit-never-saves defect is explicitly labelled a planted bug in the source comment. Recommend the bind decide **preserve-as-is vs fix** before any card is written; a parity test that expects the update to succeed would contradict the legacy.
- c08: `ARPURQTY` has no maintainer in `ATU_SRC`. Recommend an SME confirm whether the field is fed by an external purchasing process (the XML file consumer?) or is simply static sample data.
- c10 / c13: `XML` and `XSS` service programs are outside the allowlist. Recommend treating both files as **integration edges** ("a file appears at PATH") unless the room wants their internals in scope.
- c12: consider splitting `PRO203` into its own slice at bind if the report is in scope — it shares nothing with the screens except `PRO202D.FMT03`.
