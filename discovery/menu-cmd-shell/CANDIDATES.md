- c02: the three out-of-tree targets (two QM queries, one command) are the largest **functional** blind spot in the estate — two reports and the log viewer. Recommend the room ask the source owner for `CUSQRY`, `ARTQRY`, `CUSQRYFMT` and `ADSPUSRSPC` before declaring the estate scanned.
- c05 / c06: recommend the target carry the 8 live messages as a resource file; the 4 dead ones can be dropped.
- c07: `CVTSPLPDF` is a third-party / ARCAD command; treat as an integration edge (already the recommendation in `ord-print-ord500`).
