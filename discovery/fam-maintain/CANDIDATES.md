## Deferred recommendations (prose only)

- c02: the missing `FADEL` check in `ExistArtFam` is the one rule here with a business consequence (articles can be assigned to a deleted family). Recommend an SME decide whether that is intended before a card is written.
- c11 / c12: no family maintenance and a dormant `FAVATCD` suggest families are static reference data in this sample. Recommend **defer** the slice as a whole unless the ART slices are bound (they are its only consumers).
- Slice id: recommend the room either accept the misnomer or rename to `fam-modules` at bind (rename would touch `INDEX`, `APP_MANIFEST` hints and this directory).
